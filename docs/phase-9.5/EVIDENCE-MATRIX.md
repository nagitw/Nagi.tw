## Classification rules and evidence boundary

- VERIFIED: directly supported by available independent evidence for the stated narrow claim.
- PARTIALLY VERIFIED: relevant support exists, but scope, source access or outcome remains incomplete.
- UNVERIFIED: sufficient independent evidence was not available. Removing a claim is remediation, not verification.
- CONTRADICTED: actual conflicting evidence is established. None was established here; uncertainty is not contradiction.
