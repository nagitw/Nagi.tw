## MyFirstCTF 2025 removal and ordering

There was no MyFirstCTF 2025 source entry in the starting checkout. No unrelated
2025 event was deleted. The existing editorial exclusion list now explicitly
rejects `ais3-myfirstctf-2025`; source/output tests assert that it is absent and
that the 2026 identity occurs exactly once per language. An actual calendar-2025
EOF preliminary record is intentionally retained: it is not MyFirstCTF.

Years remain newest-first. Precisely dated records within each year now run
earliest-to-latest. Unknown dates follow dated records; slug ties give identical
English/Traditional Chinese ordering. Month-only precision is retained without
inventing a day. All source-file awards and participation states are preserved.
