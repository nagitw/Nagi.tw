# Phase 8 content audit

The baseline had five Markdown/MDX collections. Publication was controlled by
`status: published` in the shared collection selector. English routes were
unprefixed, Traditional Chinese routes used `/zh/`, and detail pages came from
published entries in the requested language. All baseline entries were drafts.
Home and About contained general site descriptions. Experience and Photography
contained template records only.
