# Medical IoT Trust

After a Gateway receives Medical IoT device data, what can we actually trust?

Receiving data is only the first step. I'm looking at device identity, message freshness, evidence checks, and the Gateway as a trust boundary.

CURRENT RESEARCH

## MedTrust

Medical IoT Trust & Evidence System

Research question: How can the Gateway determine that data comes from a known device, is not replayed, and has not been modified?

Threat / Trust Boundary: Unknown devices, forged or replayed messages, and evidence changed after export.

Current status: CURRENT — validating trust assumptions through repeatable experiments.
