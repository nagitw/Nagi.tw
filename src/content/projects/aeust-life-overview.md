## Context and contribution

AEUST Life brings campus activities and opportunities for student exchange into a common entry point. Its public README identifies Nagi as the initiator. It is an independent, unofficial project, with no affiliation to the school's administrative systems. This does not establish sole authorship of every component.

## Engineering foundation

The reviewed README describes an Astro and strict TypeScript frontend, a Cloudflare Workers API, shared validation, and D1 / SQLite migrations. It documents local API, validation and database testing commands. Those commands have not been re-executed as part of this portfolio update.

## Stage and limits

At the reviewed revision, V0.1 is a development foundation and has not been formally deployed. Activities, clubs, group meetups, food and second-hand pages are planning introductions, not evidence of operational services or real usage. No authentication, trading service, public feedback lookup or management console is claimed.

This case study does not claim an institutional partnership, production deployment, user count or independently verified test result.
